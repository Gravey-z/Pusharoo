How delivery allowance and expiry work

One matching event delivered to one webhook uses one unit, even if the HTTP delivery fails. One event matching three webhooks uses three units. Automatic retries retain their original charge; manual redelivery uses a new unit; test sends are free.

When allowance is exhausted or paid access ends, new deliveries wait without HTTP attempts. Already-charged retries keep their charge and still require an enabled webhook. Grace adds no days or allowance. Consecutive prepaid periods have no grace between them.

Historical recovery has a separate block-scanning quota and cooldown. Only newly queued billable deliveries consume event allowance; scanning limits do not increase with a paid package.
